Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Data Collection

To provide a functional and secure experience, Pi Explorer collects minimal data necessary for the core services:

  • Wallet Address: We collect your public Pi wallet address solely to identify your on-chain actions, such as voting and transactions within the ecosystem.
  • Language Preference: To optimize your user interface and provide a localized experience.
  • Transaction Metadata: Essential data required to verify payments and boosts via the Pi SDK.

2. Data Usage

Your privacy is our priority. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Data usage is strictly limited to:

  • Maintaining and securing your user profile.
  • Recording legitimate community votes and meritocratic rankings.
  • Preventing fraudulent activities, Sybil attacks, and ensuring ecosystem integrity.

3. Third-Party Services

Pi Explorer integrates with trusted industry-standard services to ensure security and scalability:

  • Supabase: Used for secure database management and encrypted data storage.
  • Pi Network SDK: Used for user authentication and secure, decentralized payment processing.

We encourage you to review the individual privacy policies of Supabase and the Pi Network.

4. Security

All interactions involving your assets and identity are conducted through the secure, sandboxed Pi Browser environment. Our backend architecture utilizes edge functions to validate all incoming requests, ensuring that no unauthorized data access can occur.

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact the Pi Explorer development team.
